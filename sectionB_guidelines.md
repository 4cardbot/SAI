### 2. Structure & Focus of Section B

Section B evaluates your ability to apply domain-specific sports science and physiotherapy knowledge to practical sporting contexts:

* **Practical Athletic Focus:** Questions are explicitly oriented towards **athlete support environments, high-performance training, competition, recovery, and injury management**, avoiding purely academic or laboratory-centric framing.
* **Evidence-Based Clinical Reasoning:** Emphasizes standard clinical practice guidelines (CPGs), diagnostic accuracy (sensitivity/specificity), evidence-based modalities, and structured rehabilitation progressions.
* **Interdisciplinary Integration:** Applies principles of biomechanics, kinesiology, exercise physiology, and sports medicine directly to athlete care.

### 3. Detailed Syllabus for Section B (Physiotherapy)

*(As prescribed in **Annexure A** of the official SAI Notification)*

#### **1. Human Anatomy & Kinesiology**
* **General Anatomy & Terminology:** Planes, axes, anatomical terminology, osteology, arthrology.
* **Musculoskeletal Anatomy:** Origins, insertions, innervations, blood supply, and functional actions of muscles across the upper extremity, lower extremity, pelvis, and vertebral column.
* **Neuroanatomy:** Brachial plexus, lumbosacral plexus, peripheral nerve pathways, entrapment sites, dermatomes, myotomes, deep tendon reflexes, and autonomic pathways.
* **Systemic Anatomy:** Structural anatomy relevant to high-performance athletes.

#### **2. Human Physiology & Exercise Physiology**
* **General Physiology & Homeostasis:** Fluid balance, acid-base regulation, blood and immune physiology, special senses, and thermoregulation under environmental extremes (heat, cold, altitude).
* **Nerve and Muscle Physiology:** Sliding filament theory, motor units (Henneman's size principle), neuromuscular junction transmission, excitation-contraction coupling, Type I vs. Type II fiber properties.
* **Cardiorespiratory Physiology:** Acute cardiorespiratory responses to exercise, chronic adaptations, $\text{VO}_2\text{max}$, anaerobic threshold, ventilatory thresholds, and recovery dynamics.
* **Endocrine & Metabolic Adaptations:** Hormonal regulation during exercise and tissue healing (growth hormone, IGF-1, cortisol, testosterone, insulin).

#### **3. Electrotherapy and Electrodiagnosis**
* **Fundamentals & Therapeutic Effects:** Physiological effects of electrical stimulation, heat transfer, and radiation.
* **Low & Medium Frequency Currents:** TENS (High-frequency conventional, low-frequency acupuncture-like, burst mode; pain gate theory vs. endogenous opioid release), IFT (interferential therapy, carrier/beat frequencies), Russian current, High-Voltage Pulsed Current (HVPC), Faradic/Galvanic stimulation.
* **High-Frequency & Thermal Modalities:**
  * **Therapeutic Ultrasound:** 1 MHz (deep tissues) vs. 3 MHz (superficial tissues), pulsed vs. continuous modes, duty cycles, attenuation, phonophoresis.
  * **Shortwave Diathermy (SWD):** Inductive vs. capacitive methods, physiological thermal responses.
  * **Infrared Radiation (IRR) & Ultraviolet Radiation (UVR).**
* **Advanced Modalities:** Low-Level Laser Therapy (LLLT / Photobiomodulation), Extracorporeal Shockwave Therapy (ESWT — radial vs. focused for chronic tendinopathies/plantar fasciitis), Cryotherapy (phases of cryotherapy, cryokinetics, contrast baths).
* **Biofeedback & Electrodiagnosis:** Strength-Duration (S-D) curves, chronaxie, rheobase, EMG biofeedback for neuromuscular re-education.
* **Clinical Indications & Strict Contraindications:** Pacemakers, active malignancy, acute hemorrhage, deep vein thrombosis, pregnancy, open epiphyseal plates, metallic implants.

#### **4. Exercise Therapy and Rehabilitation**
* **Fundamentals of Exercise Therapy:** Overload, progression, specificity, individualization, open vs. closed kinetic chain exercises.
* **Stretching & Flexibility:** Static stretching, dynamic stretching, ballistic stretching, and Proprioceptive Neuromuscular Facilitation (PNF) techniques (Hold-Relax, Contract-Relax, Agonist Contraction, Rhythmic Stabilization).
* **Strengthening Regimens:** Progressive Resistance Training (PRT), isometric, concentric, and **eccentric loading protocols** (Alfredson, Silbernagel, heavy slow resistance for tendinopathy management).
* **Manual Therapy & Mobilization:** Maitland joint mobilization grades (Grades I to V / thrust manipulation), Kaltenborn traction/glide grades, Mulligan Mobilization with Movement (MWM).
* **Neuromuscular & Functional Training:** Proprioceptive training, perturbation training, plyometrics, hydrotherapy/aquatic therapy, therapeutic massage techniques, and kinesiotherapy.

#### **5. Biomechanics and Movement Analysis**
* **Biophysics & Mechanical Principles:** Levers (1st, 2nd, 3rd class in musculoskeletal systems), torque, moment arms, center of gravity (COG), base of support (BOS), ground reaction forces (GRF).
* **Joint Biomechanics:**
  * **Shoulder Complex:** Scapulohumeral rhythm (2:1 ratio), force couples (deltoid-rotator cuff, upper/lower trapezius-serratus anterior), subacromial impingement mechanics.
  * **Knee Joint:** Screw-home mechanism, Q-angle, patellofemoral joint contact stress at varying flexion angles (open vs. closed chain differences).
  * **Spine & Pelvis:** Lumbopelvic rhythm, anterior/posterior pelvic tilt, sacral nutation/counternutation, spinal load distribution.
  * **Foot & Ankle:** Subtalar pronation/supination, windlass mechanism, kinetic chain alignment.
* **Gait & Running Analysis:**
  * Complete sub-phases of gait (Perry's classification: Initial Contact, Loading Response, Mid-stance, Terminal Stance, Pre-swing, Initial Swing, Mid-swing, Terminal Swing).
  * Peak kinematics, joint moments, and EMG muscle activation patterns during each phase.
  * Running and sprinting mechanics (acceleration vs. top-speed mechanics, anterior pelvic tilt influence on hamstring strains).
* **Sports-Specific Skill Biomechanics:** Overhead throwing/pitching kinematic phases (wind-up, early cocking, late cocking, acceleration, deceleration, follow-through), jumping/landing mechanics (valgus collapse, Landing Error Scoring System - LESS).

#### **6. Assessment, Diagnosis and Clinical Reasoning**
* **Systematic Assessment Framework:** Comprehensive subjective/objective examination, SOAP notes, active/passive ROM assessment, end-feels (hard, soft, firm, empty, springy), manual muscle testing (MMT Oxford scale 0–5).
* **Evidence-Based Special Orthopaedic Tests (Diagnostic Accuracy):**
  * **Knee:** Lachman test (highest sensitivity for ACL), Pivot Shift test (highest specificity for ACL), Anterior/Posterior Drawer, McMurray test, Thessaly test, Apley's test, Dial test.
  * **Shoulder:** Neer & Hawkins-Kennedy (impingement), Empty Can / Full Can (supraspinatus), Hornblower's / Infraspinatus test, Speed's & Yergason's (biceps tendon), O'Brien's test (SLAP lesion), Apprehension & Relocation test (anterior instability).
  * **Ankle & Foot:** Anterior Drawer test (ATFL), Talar Tilt test (CFL), Thompson / Simmonds squeeze test (Achilles tendon rupture), Windlass test.
  * **Spine & Pelvis:** Straight Leg Raise (SLR) / Lasègue test, Slump test, Crossed SLR, FABER (Patrick's) test, Gaenslen's test, Stork standing test.
  * **Elbow, Wrist & Hand:** Cozen's, Mill's, Maudsley's (lateral epicondylalgia), Golfer's elbow test, Finkelstein test (de Quervain's tenosynovitis), Phalen's & Tinel's tests (carpal tunnel).
* **Neurodynamics & Neural Tension:** Upper Limb Tension Tests (ULTT 1, 2a, 2b, 3 for Median, Radial, Ulnar nerve biases), neurodynamic mobilization (sliders vs. tensioners).
* **Validated Outcome Measures:** NPRS, VAS, VISA-A / VISA-P (Achilles/patellar tendinopathy), KOOS, DASH/QuickDASH, LEFS, IKDC.

#### **7. Sports Physiotherapy, Injury Management & Return to Play (RTP)**
* **Acute Soft Tissue Injury Management:** Evolution of acute protocols: **PEACE & LOVE** and **POLICE** (Protection, Optimal Loading, Ice, Compression, Elevation) vs. old RICE protocols.
* **Common Sports Pathologies & Evidence-Based Protocols:**
  * Hamstring strain injuries (biceps femoris long head vs. semimembranosus; lengthening vs. isometric rehabilitation).
  * ACL reconstruction rehabilitation (BTB vs. Hamstring vs. Quad tendon grafts; open vs. closed kinetic chain safety zones, progression to plyometrics).
  * Rotator cuff tears & Subacromial Pain Syndrome (SAPS).
  * Medial Tibial Stress Syndrome (MTSS), stress fractures (high-risk vs. low-risk anatomical sites), chronic exertional compartment syndrome.
  * Ankle sprains (lateral ligament complex: ATFL > CFL > PTFL; syndesmotic / high ankle sprains).
  * Groin pain in athletes (Doha consensus taxonomy: adductor-related, iliopsoas-related, inguinal-related, pubic-related).
* **Return to Sport / Return to Play (RTP) Frameworks:**
  * Objective exit criteria (Limb Symmetry Index $\ge 90\%$, single-leg hop test battery, Y-balance test, reactive strength index, psychological readiness via ACL-RSI).
* **Injury Prevention Programs:** FIFA 11+, Neuromuscular warm-ups, Nordic hamstring exercise, Copenhagen adduction protocol.
* **Pre-Participation Examination (PPE) & Screening:** Functional Movement Screen (FMS), dynamic posture screening, joint hypermobility (Beighton score).

#### **8. Sports Emergencies, On-Field Management & Travelling**
* **Emergency Action Plans (EAP) & On-Field Triage:** Primary survey ($<C>ABCDE$), automated external defibrillator (AED) usage, airway adjuncts.
* **Sports Concussion Protocols:** Concussion in Sport Group consensus guidelines (**SCAT6 / Child SCAT6**), on-field red flags, graduated return-to-learn and return-to-play protocols.
* **Spinal Trauma & Immobilization:** Cervical spine stabilization, log-roll techniques, scoop stretcher and spine board protocols.
* **Environmental Emergencies:** Exertional Heat Stroke (EHS — cold water immersion), heat exhaustion, hypothermia, altitude illness.
* **Team Travel Protocols:** Medical kit preparation, jet lag mitigation, compression garments for travel, hotel recovery setups.

#### **9. Sports Medicine, Pharmacology, Nutrition & Research Basics**
* **Anti-Doping Regulations (WADA / NADA):** Prohibited classes (Anabolic agents, Beta-2 agonists, peptide hormones, glucocorticoids), Therapeutic Use Exemptions (TUE), supplement contamination risks.
* **Sports Nutrition for Rehabilitation:** Protein intake recommendations for muscle synthesis during injury immobilization/rehab ($1.6 - 2.5\text{ g/kg/day}$), creatine, vitamin D, collagen/vitamin C supplementation for tendon healing.
* **Research Methodology & Evidence Levels:** Evidence hierarchy (Systematic reviews/Meta-analyses $\rightarrow$ RCTs $\rightarrow$ Cohort $\rightarrow$ Case-control $\rightarrow$ Case series), statistical significance ($p < 0.05$), confidence intervals, sensitivity, specificity, likelihood ratios.

### 5. High-Yield Strategy & Blueprint for Section B (Scoring 35+/40)

1. **Prioritize Evidence-Based Consensus Over Outdated Dogmas:**
   * *Example:* Therapeutic ultrasound for chronic non-specific low back pain shows **limited or no clinically meaningful benefit** (as demonstrated in SAI's sample paper).
   * *Example:* Eccentric loading / Heavy slow resistance is the gold standard for **tendinopathies**, whereas passive stretching alone is ineffective.
2. **Master Diagnostic Test Accuracy Metrics:**
   * Memorize which test has the highest **sensitivity** (e.g., *Lachman test* for ACL rupture) versus highest **specificity** (e.g., *Pivot Shift test* for ACL rupture).
3. **Know Exact Gait Sub-Phases & Kinematics:**
   * Be crystal clear on joint positions and active muscle contractions in every sub-phase of walking and running gait (e.g., *peak knee flexion of $\sim 60^\circ$ occurs during the Initial Swing phase*).
4. **Learn On-Field Concussion & Emergency Protocols:**
   * Memorize the latest **SCAT6** red flags (neck pain, double vision, weakness/tingling in arms/legs, severe headache, seizure, deteriorating consciousness) requiring immediate emergency hospital transfer.
5. **Manage Negative Marking:**
   * Since each wrong answer deducts **0.25 marks**, eliminate options systematically using anatomical and biomechanical reasoning rather than random guessing.
