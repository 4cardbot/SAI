# The Unified Gut-Skin Restoration Protocol

This comprehensive guide integrates the clinical functional medicine approaches to systematically seal the gut lining, rebalance the microbiome, calm systemic immune hyper-reactivity, and protect the skin barrier.

---

## Pillar 1: Gut Barrier Repair & Microbiome Balance
The gut lining directly dictates immune reactivity. If the intestinal barrier is permeable ("leaky gut"), inflammatory particles enter the bloodstream, triggering immune cascades that manifest as skin flares.

### 1. Intestinal Barrier Reinforcement
* **L-Glutamine:** 5g daily (mixed in room-temperature water on an empty stomach) to provide the primary fuel required by enterocytes to seal tight junctions in the intestinal wall.
* **Bovine Colostrum or Serum-Derived IgG:** Delivers immunoglobulins and growth factors to bind inflammatory triggers in the digestive tract and accelerate mucosal tissue repair.
* **Collagen Peptides:** 10–15g daily to provide the structural amino acids (glycine, proline) necessary for gut lining integrity.

### 2. Microbial Reseeding & Balance
* **Broad-Spectrum Multi-Strain Probiotics:** 20–100 Billion CFUs daily (taken with or right before a meal) containing diverse *Lactobacillus* and *Bifidobacterium* strains to restore healthy microbial balance.
* **Prebiotic Soluble Fiber:** Larch Arabinogalactans or gentle fiber sources (e.g., partially hydrolyzed guar gum, cooked/cooled oats, asparagus) to nourish beneficial gut bacteria.
* **Targeted Microbial Balance (Optional/As Needed):** If fungal/yeast dysbiosis or bacterial overgrowth is suspected, short courses of enteric-coated **Oil of Oregano** or **Berberine** act as broad-spectrum natural antimicrobials.

### 3. Digestive Support & Motility
* **Full-Spectrum Digestive Enzymes:** 1 capsule taken at the start of substantial meals to ensure complete breakdown of proteins and fats, preventing undigested particles from irritating the gut.
* **Natural Motility Activators:** Ginger root extract or artichoke leaf extract taken between meals to support the Migrating Motor Complex (MMC), preventing bacterial stagnation.

## Pillar 2: Systemic Inflammation & Immune Balancing
Psoriasis is fundamentally driven by immune signaling pathways. This tier focuses on regulating immune responses and neutralizing oxidative stress.

### 1. Primary Immune Regulators
* **Vitamin D3 + Vitamin K2 (MK-7):** 2,000–5,000 IU of D3 combined with 100mcg of K2 daily (taken with a fat-containing meal) to modulate T-cell activity while directing calcium safely into bones.
* **High-Potency Omega-3 Fish Oil:** 1,000–2,000mg combined EPA/DHA daily to suppress pro-inflammatory eicosanoids and cytokine cascades.
* **Evening Primrose Oil (EPO):** 500–1,000mg daily to supply Gamma-Linolenic Acid (GLA), targeted specifically at skin-barrier repair and local anti-inflammatory signaling.

### 2. Cellular Detoxification & Antioxidants
* **NAC (N-Acetyl Cysteine):** 600mg 1–2 times daily to break down microbial biofilms and serve as the direct precursor to Glutathione.
* **Liposomal Glutathione:** 250–500mg daily (or sustained via NAC) to neutralize systemic oxidative stress and support liver clearance pathways.
* **Alpha-Lipoic Acid (ALA):** 200–300mg daily to regenerate other antioxidants (Vitamins C and E) and protect nerve and skin tissue.
* **Buffered Vitamin C & Zinc Picolinate:** 500–1,000mg Vitamin C with 15–30mg Zinc for tissue repair and collagen synthesis.

### 3. Nervous System & Nighttime Anti-Itch Support
* **Magnesium Glycinate / Multi-Form Magnesium:** 300–400mg taken 45 minutes before sleep to lower cortisol, relax peripheral nerves, and reduce nocturnal itching impulses.
* **Methylated B-Complex:** Taken in the morning with breakfast to support cellular energy pathways and neurotransmitter synthesis during recovery.

## Pillar 3: The Functional Elimination & Anti-Inflammatory Diet
Supplements cannot outpace continuous dietary irritation. This protocol focuses on removing primary antigenic foods while loading the body with nutrient-dense building blocks.

| Category | Foods to Strictly Eliminate | Foods to Heavily Emphasize |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grains & Starches** | Gluten-containing grains (wheat, barley, rye), refined white flour | Sweet potatoes, wild rice, quinoa, cassava, cooked oats |
| **Dairy** | All conventional cow's milk, cheeses, creams, and milk chocolates | Unsweetened coconut milk, almond milk, ghee (clarified butter) |
| **Proteins** | Processed deli meats, factory-farmed high-fat meats | Wild-caught salmon, pasture-raised eggs (if tolerated), bone broth |
| **Produce** | Sugary fruit juices, high-sugar dried fruits | Cruciferous vegetables (broccoli, cabbage), dark leafy greens, wild blueberries |
| **Fats & Oils** | Industrial seed oils (canola, corn, soybean, sunflower), trans fats | Extra virgin olive oil, avocado oil, whole avocados, raw pumpkin seeds |
| **Lifestyle** | Refined white sugars, artificial sweeteners, alcohol, excess caffeine | Pure water, organic herbal teas (chamomile, peppermint, ginger) |

### Key Meal Habits
* **Structured Meal Spacing:** Maintain a 3.5- to 4-hour window between meals without snacking to allow the digestive tract's cleaning waves (MMC) to sweep debris out of the small intestine.
* **Hydration Window:** Drink the bulk of your water between meals rather than chugging large amounts during meals to avoid diluting stomach acid and digestive enzymes.

## Pillar 4: Lifestyle, Nervous System & External Care
* **Stress Modulation:** Engage in 10 minutes of diaphragmatic breathing, box breathing, or gentle mindfulness daily. High cortisol directly degrades the intestinal mucus lining.
* **Circulation & Lymphatic Flow:** Engage in 20–30 minutes of low-impact walking or gentle stretching daily to ensure oxygen and nutrients circulate to peripheral skin capillaries without inducing physical trauma.
* **External Skin Protection:**
  * Cleanse with mild, soap-free, pH-balanced washes in lukewarm water (avoid hot showers).
  * Lock in hydration within 3 minutes of bathing using simple, ceramide-rich, fragrance-free emollients on damp skin.
  * Avoid physical friction, aggressive scrubbing, or scratching to prevent trauma-induced lesions (Koebner phenomenon).
