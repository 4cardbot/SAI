### 2. Structure & Weightage of Section A

Section A is **common to all sports science disciplines** (40% of the total exam score). It is designed to evaluate your interdisciplinary understanding across sports science and general sports awareness:

* **Total Marks:** **40 Marks** (40 MCQs)
* **Section A1: Basic Sports Science Knowledge (32 Marks / 32 MCQs)** — Cross-disciplinary core scientific principles.
* **Section A2: General Sports Awareness (8 Marks / 8 MCQs)** — Indian and international sports ecosystem, governance, and anti-doping.

---

### 3. Detailed Syllabus for Section A

#### **Part A1: Basic Sports Science Knowledge (32 Marks)**
1. **Core Disciplines Foundational Concepts:**
   * **Physiotherapy:** Injury mechanisms, acute care protocols, return-to-play criteria, basic rehabilitation principles.
   * **Biomechanics:** Movement analysis, linear and angular kinematics/kinetics, force vectors, levers, projectile motion, gait analysis.
   * **Exercise Physiology:** Bioenergetics, ATP-PC, glycolytic and aerobic systems, lactate dynamics, cardiorespiratory adaptations to acute and chronic exercise.
   * **Strength & Conditioning:** Overload, progression, specificity, periodization models, load variables (volume, intensity, density).
   * **Anthropometry & Somatotyping:** Body composition methods (skinfolds, BIA, DEXA), somatotypes (Heath-Carter: Endomorphy, Mesomorphy, Ectomorphy), biological maturation.
   * **Sports Nutrition:** Macronutrients/micronutrients, energy availability, hydration assessment, pre/intra/post-exercise fueling.
   * **Biochemistry:** Key performance and recovery biomarkers (Creatine Kinase, Urea, Cortisol, Testosterone, Ferritin).
   * **Sports Psychology:** Motivation theories, cognitive vs. somatic anxiety, arousal control, self-regulation, mental toughness.

2. **Testing Parameters, Standardization & Athlete Assessment:**
   * Validity, reliability, sensitivity, and specificity of sports testing batteries.
   * Field vs. Laboratory testing protocols.
   * Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), instrument calibration, safety contraindications, and informed consent.
   * Testing guidelines from international bodies (ACSM, NSCA, IOC, FIFA, World Athletics).

3. **Data Interpretation & Applied Analytics:**
   * Interpreting test results for athletic profiling, identifying limiting factors.
   * Translating scientific data into practical coaching and training recommendations.
   * Longitudinal performance tracking and reporting.

4. **Performance Enhancement & Recovery Protocols:**
   * Training interventions for strength, speed, power, endurance, and agility.
   * Active vs. passive recovery methods (cryotherapy, thermotherapy, massage, pneumatic compression, hydrotherapy).
   * Sleep hygiene, circadian rhythm management, and fatigue monitoring tools.

5. **Emerging Technologies & Biostatistics:**
   * Wearables, GPS tracking, Inertial Measurement Units (IMUs), force plates, video analysis systems.
   * AI/ML and Athlete Management Systems (AMS) in elite sport; ethical and data privacy issues.
   * Descriptive & inferential statistics (mean, SD, t-tests, ANOVA, correlation, regression, effect size).

6. **Holistic Development, Travel & Ethics:**
   * Long-Term Athlete Development (LTAD) models, overtraining syndrome and burnout prevention.
   * Jet lag management, travel fatigue, altitude and heat acclimatization strategies.
   * Yoga and mind-body interventions (Pranayama, mindfulness, mobility integration).

---

#### **Part A2: General Sports Awareness (8 Marks)**
1. **Olympic & Asian Games:** History, editions, medal tallies, record-holders, future host cities, Indian performance.
2. **Indian Sports Ecosystem & Government Schemes:**
   * **Khelo India Scheme** (Verticals, Khelo India Youth/University/Para Games).
   * **Target Olympic Podium Scheme (TOPS)** and Development Group.
   * Roles of SAI, Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports (MYAS), National Sports Federations (NSFs), and IOA.
3. **Anti-Doping Regulations & Clean Sport:**
   * World Anti-Doping Agency (**WADA**) Code and Prohibited Substances List.
   * National Anti-Doping Agency (**NADA**) structure and anti-doping violations.
   * Therapeutic Use Exemptions (**TUE**), testing protocols, and biological passport.
4. **Major International Championships:** Commonwealth Games, World Cups, and World Championships in major Olympic sports.
5. **Sports Governance, Ethics, and Safeguarding:** Athlete welfare, POSH guidelines, and code of conduct.

### 5. High-Yield Preparation Strategy for Physiotherapy Applicants

1. **Think Beyond Clinical Treatment:** Section A focuses on **sports performance and high-performance environments** rather than hospital bed-bound pathology. Emphasize athletic performance metrics (HRV, GPS load, rate of force development, RED-S).
2. **Prioritize Objective Calculations:** Be prepared for formula-based questions often asked in Section A/B (such as Mid-parental height estimation, target weight from body fat percentage, Waist-to-Hip Ratio, and fluid replacement calculations).
3. **Master Anti-Doping & Government Schemes:** These are straightforward scoring areas in the 8-mark Section A2 that take minimal study time.
4. **Accuracy Over Guesswork:** With a **0.25 negative marking penalty** per wrong question and tie-breaker criteria giving weight to Section A/A1 scores, avoid blind guessing.
