Question:
The Khelo India Programme was launched with which primary objective?

Candidate selected: D
Response-sheet keyed option: A
Correct option: D — Revival of sports culture at the grassroots level across India

Evidence summary:
The official Press Information Bureau release states that Khelo India was introduced to revive sports culture in India at the grass-root level. This directly supports option D, not elite-only financial support.
