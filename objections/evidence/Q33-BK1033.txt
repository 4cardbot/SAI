Question 33 | Item code BK1033

Question:
Which body is the supreme authority of the Olympic Movement globally?

Candidate selected: C
Response-sheet keyed option: B
Correct option: C — International Olympic Committee (IOC)

Evidence summary:
Chapter 1 of the Olympic Charter explicitly identifies the International Olympic Committee as the supreme authority of the Olympic Movement. Therefore option C is correct.
