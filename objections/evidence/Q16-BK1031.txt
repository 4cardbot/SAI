Question 16 | Item code BK1031

Question:
Overtraining syndrome in athletes is best characterized by:

Candidate selected: C
Response-sheet keyed option: B
Correct option: C — Prolonged maladaptation and performance decline despite adequate rest, often with mood and hormonal disturbances

Evidence summary:
The ECSS/ACSM consensus statement identifies prolonged maladaptation, performance decline, fatigue and mood disturbance as characteristic of overtraining syndrome, distinguishing it from short-term functional overreaching. Therefore option C is correct.

Source:
Prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of the overtraining syndrome — joint consensus statement
Meeusen et al., Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise, 2013; PubMed
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23247672/
