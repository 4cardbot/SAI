Question 11 | Item code BK1001

Question:
Which somatotype, as classified by Sheldon, is characterized by a lean, linear body build with low fat and muscle mass?

Candidate selected: B
Response-sheet keyed option: D
Correct option: B — Ectomorph

Evidence summary:
The NCBI/National Academies source identifies Sheldon et al.'s three somatotype extremes, including ectomorph. The SAI NSNIS material describes ectomorphs as having thin arms and legs and little muscle and fat. Therefore option B is correct.

Source:
Body Composition and Military Performance — National Academies/NCBI, PDF p.67
