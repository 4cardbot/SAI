SAI RECRUITMENT 2026 — OBJECTION EVIDENCE
Question 37 | Item code BK1035

Question:
Which of the following is prohibited at ALL TIMES (both in-competition and out-of-competition) under the WADA Prohibited List?

Candidate selected: B
Response-sheet keyed option: D
Correct option: B — Erythropoietin (EPO)

Evidence summary:
The WADA 2026 Prohibited List places erythropoietins, including EPO, in section S2, “Prohibited at all times (in- and out-of-competition).” Therefore option B is correct.

Source:
2026 Prohibited List — section S2.1.1
World Anti-Doping Agency
https://www.wada-ama.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/2026list_en_final_clean_september_2025.pdf

Complaint:
WADA 2026 List p.1, S2.1: EPO is under substances prohibited at all times. Option B is correct, not D.
