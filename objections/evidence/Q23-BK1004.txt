Question 23 | Item code BK1004

Question:
Peripheral muscle fatigue during high-intensity exercise is most closely associated with accumulation of:

Candidate selected: A
Response-sheet keyed option: B
Correct option: A — Hydrogen ions (H+) and inorganic phosphate

Evidence summary:
Exercise physiology literature identifies hydrogen ions and inorganic phosphate as important metabolites associated with high-intensity muscle fatigue. A recent human study measured both and found intramuscular inorganic phosphate strongly related to peripheral fatigue. Among the listed options, A is the only option containing these established fatigue-associated metabolites.

Source:
Hureau et al., Journal of Physiology, 2022; PubMed
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35593645/
