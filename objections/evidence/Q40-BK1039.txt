Question 40 | Item code BK1039

Question:
How many interlocking rings does the Olympic flag feature, and what do they collectively represent?

Candidate selected: B
Response-sheet keyed option: A
Correct option: B — 5 rings — five continents of the world united by the Olympic Movement

Evidence summary:
The IOC Olympic Brand Centre describes the Olympic symbol as five interlocking rings representing the union of the five continents and the meeting of athletes from around the world. Therefore option B is correct.

Source:
Olympic Symbol
International Olympic Committee Olympic Brand Centre
https://brand-centre.olympic.org/olympic_symbol

Complaint:
IOC Olympic Symbol page 3: five rings represent five parts of the world and athletes from all five continents. Option B is correct.
