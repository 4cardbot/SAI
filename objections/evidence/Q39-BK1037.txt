SAI RECRUITMENT 2026 — OBJECTION EVIDENCE
Question 39 | Item code BK1037

Question:
The National Sports Development Code of India, 2011 mandates that office-bearers of National Sports Federations must not exceed which age limit?

Candidate selected: A
Response-sheet keyed option: C
Correct option: A — 70 years

Evidence summary:
The Government of India states that the President, Secretary and Treasurer of a recognised National Sports Federation, including the Indian Olympic Association, cease to hold office on attaining 70 years of age. Therefore option A is correct.

Source:
Age and tenure limits for office bearers of National Sports Federations
Press Information Bureau, Government of India
