Question:
The Inverted-U hypothesis in sports psychology suggests that performance is optimal when arousal is:

Candidate selected: C
Response-sheet keyed option: B
Correct option: C — At a moderate, optimal level

Evidence summary:
The Inverted-U / Yerkes-Dodson model describes poor performance at very low or very high arousal and the best performance at a moderate, optimal level. Therefore option C is correct.
