# A2 — Indian Sports Ecosystem & Government Schemes

## Institution–scheme map

```text
Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports (MYAS) → national policy, schemes and government oversight
  │
  ├─ Sports Authority of India (SAI) → implementation, centres, coaching, sports science and athlete support
  ├─ Target Olympic Podium Scheme (TOPS)/Mission Olympic Cell → targeted elite-athlete support
  ├─ Khelo India → participation, competitions, talent and facilities
  └─ National Sports Development Fund (NSDF) → grants/resources supporting sport and elite preparation

Indian Olympic Association (IOA) + recognised National Sports Federations (NSFs) → Olympic representation and sport-specific administration
National Centres of Excellence (NCOEs) → high-performance training + integrated sports-science support
```

## Scheme flash table

| Name | Purpose / recognition cue |
| --- | --- |
| **Khelo India** | Broad-based development: sports participation, competitions, talent identification, infrastructure, centres/academies, inclusion and community access |
| **Target Olympic Podium Scheme (TOPS)** | Targeted support for athletes with realistic Olympic/Paralympic/major-Games potential; Mission Olympic Cell and technical support are central cues |
| **National Sports Development Fund (NSDF)** | Mobilises financial support for sports development and high-performance needs such as training, travel and equipment |
| **Sports Authority of India (SAI)** | Implements athlete-development and high-performance programmes; provides coaching, facilities, sports science, medical/rehabilitation and support services |
| **National Centres of Excellence (NCOEs)** | National high-performance centres with sport-specific training and multidisciplinary support—coaches, sports science, medicine, nutrition and allied services |
| **Indian Olympic Association (IOA)** | India’s National Olympic Committee; national representation and Olympic Movement interface |
| **National Sports Federation (NSF)** | Governs and develops a particular sport in India; rules, selection and competition administration are sport-specific |

## How to solve scheme questions

```text
Problem in stem → identify scale → choose scheme
participation/facilities/talent pipeline → Khelo India
elite medal pathway/individual support → TOPS
high-performance campus/integrated support → NCOE / SAI
funding/resource mobilisation → NSDF
national representation → IOA
sport-specific selection/rules → NSF
```

## Governance distinctions

- The **Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports** is the policy/administrative ministry; it is not the same as the **Sports Authority of India**.
- The **Sports Authority of India** is an implementing and athlete-support organisation; do not reduce it to only coaching.
- A **scheme** is not a constitutional sports body.
- A recognised **National Sports Federation** is not automatically the **Indian Olympic Association** and does not replace the International Olympic Committee or an International Federation.
- Check the scheme’s official operational period and current guidelines; scheme components can be revised.

## Easy ways to remember it

- **Khelo India is broad, TOPS is targeted, an NCOE is a high-performance centre, and the National Sports Development Fund is a funding route.**
- **The Sports Authority of India implements programmes, the Indian Olympic Association represents India, and a National Sports Federation runs one sport.**

## Self-test

1. A district needs facilities and talent identification. Which scheme family is the closest fit?
2. An Olympic medal prospect needs targeted international preparation. Which programme is the likely cue?
3. Who is the national Olympic representative body, and who governs a particular sport?
4. Why is an NCOE different from a generic local sports facility?
