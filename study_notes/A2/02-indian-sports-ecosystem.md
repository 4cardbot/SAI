# A2 — Indian Sports Ecosystem & Government Schemes

## Institution–scheme map

```text
MYAS  → national policy, schemes and government oversight
  │
  ├─ SAI  → implementation, centres, coaching, sports science and athlete support
  ├─ TOPS/Mission Olympic Cell → targeted elite-athlete support
  ├─ Khelo India → participation, competitions, talent and facilities
  └─ NSDF → grants/resources supporting sport and elite preparation

IOA + recognised NSFs → Olympic representation and sport-specific administration
NCOEs → high-performance training + integrated sports-science support
```

## Scheme flash table

| Name | Purpose / recognition cue |
| --- | --- |
| **Khelo India** | Broad-based development: sports participation, competitions, talent identification, infrastructure, centres/academies, inclusion and community access |
| **TOPS** | Targeted support for athletes with realistic Olympic/Paralympic/major-Games potential; Mission Olympic Cell and technical support are central cues |
| **NSDF** | Mobilises financial support for sports development and high-performance needs such as training, travel and equipment |
| **SAI** | Implements athlete-development and high-performance programmes; provides coaching, facilities, sports science, medical/rehabilitation and support services |
| **NCOE** | National high-performance centres with sport-specific training and multidisciplinary support—coaches, sports science, medicine, nutrition and allied services |
| **IOA** | India’s National Olympic Committee; national representation and Olympic Movement interface |
| **NSF** | Governs and develops a particular sport in India; rules, selection and competition administration are sport-specific |

## How to solve scheme questions

```text
Problem in stem → identify scale → choose scheme
participation/facilities/talent pipeline → Khelo India
elite medal pathway/individual support → TOPS
high-performance campus/integrated support → NCOE / SAI
funding/resource mobilisation → NSDF
national representation → IOA
sport-specific selection/rules → NSF
```

## Governance distinctions

- **MYAS** is the policy/administrative ministry; it is not the same as SAI.
- **SAI** is an implementing and athlete-support organisation; do not reduce it to only coaching.
- A **scheme** is not a constitutional sports body.
- A **recognised NSF** is not automatically the IOA and does not replace the IOC/IF structure.
- Check the scheme’s official operational period and current guidelines; scheme components can be revised.

## Memory tactics

- **Khelo = many; TOPS = top; NCOE = centre; NSDF = funds; SAI = implements; IOA = India at Olympics; NSF = one sport.**
- Draw the map from **policy → implementation → athlete → event** before answering any matching question.

## Self-test

1. A district needs facilities and talent identification. Which scheme family is the closest fit?
2. An Olympic medal prospect needs targeted international preparation. Which programme is the likely cue?
3. Who is the national Olympic representative body, and who governs a particular sport?
4. Why is an NCOE different from a generic local sports facility?
